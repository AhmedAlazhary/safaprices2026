About Safa Transport

Company Profile

Safa Transport is a privately owned and managed family enterprise dedicated to provide full integrated services to the Egyptian and Foreign Companies in Egypt, with a reputation for providing outstanding quality that continues to secure a loyal customer base.

As a customer-focused company, our team is committed not only to meeting but exceeding our customers' expectations at all times, through specialized services tailored to their needs. This includes all aspects of transportation through highly professional cadres.

Our Mission

The goal of Safa Transport is to set the standards for industries we serve and to continually strive for excellence in all that we do. The following principles dictate our actions as we continue our quest for excellence:

  • Strive to achieve a company-wide commitment to continuous improvement in all areas of our business.
  • Maintain the highest standards of honesty and integrity in all relationships with our employees, customers, suppliers, service groups and community.
  • Utilize the best technologies in mechanical, statistical and management techniques to effectively bring high quality services to the marketplace and to ensure employee and customer satisfaction.
